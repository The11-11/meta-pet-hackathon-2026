Use species, age, owner-reported needs and the current diet to make feeding guidance relevant. Treat breed as context, not a feeding prescription. Ask for weight, current food and the veterinarian's plan before discussing portions. Do not invent calorie targets, portion sizes, supplements or therapeutic diets. Distinguish an unrecorded meal from an uneaten meal. Respect food sensitivities without diagnosing them; dietary or medical changes belong with the veterinarian.
